About this business

Thirty Second Lead provides AI lead-response and appointment-setting services to cosmetic and dental clinics in Australia. The service places outbound calls to a client clinic's own enquiries and existing contacts on that clinic's behalf, qualifies them, and books appointments into the clinic's calendar.

The website thirtysecondlead.com is owned and operated by Lucas Putera Somadjaja under the registered business name Thirty Second Lead.

Calling practices & compliance

Calls are made in accordance with Australian telemarketing regulations, including the Do Not Call Register Act 2006 and the Telecommunications (Telemarketing and Research Calls) Industry Standard. This means valid calling line identification on every call, calling only within permitted hours, an immediate opt-out on request, and washing numbers against the Do Not Call Register before dialling.

The automated assistant identifies itself as an automated assistant at the start of every call and will transfer to a human on request.

To opt out of contact, or to request that your details be removed, email hello@thirtysecondlead.com and the request will be actioned promptly.

Privacy

Personal information collected through this website and through calls is used solely to respond to enquiries and to provide the service described above. It is not sold or disclosed to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information held about you, email hello@thirtysecondlead.com.
