What we do with your data.

In plain English, without the legal padding. If you're a practice manager deciding whether to let us near your patient enquiries, this is the page to read.

  • We only ever touch the enquiries a clinic sends us. We never buy, scrape or rent contact lists.
  • Patient details belong to the clinic. We hold them to do the job and for nothing else.
  • We don't sell data, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing. Not ever.

What we collect

Two different sets of people, so it's worth separating them.

WhoWhat
A patient who enquires Their name, phone number and email as they typed it into the clinic's form, which treatment they asked about, and a recording and written transcript of the call our assistant makes to them. Plus the outcome — answered, booked, asked not to be called again.
Clinic staff Name, work email and phone, the clinic's business details, and calendar access so appointments can be written into it.
Anyone using this website Whatever you type into the contact form or the live demo. The demo dials the number you give it — that's the whole point of it — and that call is treated exactly like a patient call.

Why we hold it

  • To make the callback the patient asked for, and to answer their questions accurately.
  • To book the appointment into the clinic's calendar and send the confirmation.
  • To give the clinic its monthly report, and to show our own working if a call is ever disputed.
  • To honour an opt-out — which means keeping just enough to make sure we don't call that person again.

That's the complete list. We don't use patient details to advertise to them, and we don't use one clinic's data to help another.

How long we keep it

Honestly: I haven't set final retention periods yet. I'd rather tell you that than publish a tidy number I haven't thought through and then quietly ignore it.

What I can tell you today is the principle. We keep what we need to run the service and to show our working if a call is ever disputed — and nothing beyond that. You can ask us to delete your records at any time, and we will. When a clinic stops working with us, we hand back or delete their records on request; we don't keep a copy to sell to the clinic down the road.

The one deliberate exception is opt-outs. If you've told us not to call you, we keep that record — deleting it is exactly how someone ends up being called twice.

Once the periods are set they'll be published here, and the clinics we work with will hear about it before anything changes.

What you can ask for

Whether you're a patient who got a call or a clinic that hired us, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you exactly what we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that's wrong.
  • Delete it.
  • Stop contacting you — no questions asked.

Email hello@thirtysecondlead.com. It comes to a real person, and we'll come back to you as fast as I can. You don't have to explain why, and asking costs nothing.

If we get it wrong and you're not satisfied with how we've handled it, you can take it to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au. We'd rather you came to us first, but that route exists and we're not going to hide it.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels over encrypted connections and sits behind accounts that only we can reach. Access is limited to the people who need it to run the service — which today is one person. This website itself holds no database: nothing you type here is stored on it.

If something ever does go wrong in a way that could put someone at risk, we'll tell the affected clinics and the Information Commissioner, as the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme requires.
