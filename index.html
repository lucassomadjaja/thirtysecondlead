FAQ

The honest answers

Is it really an AI?
Yes. The assistant always identifies itself as an automated assistant and can hand the call to a real person whenever the patient asks. It's designed to be helpful, not to trick anyone.
Is this compliant in Australia?
Yes. Calls follow ACMA's telemarketing rules — valid caller ID, permitted calling hours, and an immediate opt-out — and numbers are washed against the Do Not Call Register before we dial.
What does it cost?
The pilot on your existing leads is free. After that it's a simple flat monthly fee with no lock-in. Book a quick call and I'll give you exact numbers for your clinic.
What if a patient wants to speak to a human?
The assistant offers a human hand-off and books a callback with your team — so no one is ever stuck talking to a bot they don't want to.
Do I need to change my systems?
No. It books into your current calendar and works with the lead sources you already use. Setup is done for you.
How quickly can we be live?
Usually within a week. Most of that is provisioning your clinic's phone number, which carriers review before it goes active.
