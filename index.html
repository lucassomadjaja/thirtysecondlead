FAQ

The honest answers

Is it really an AI?
Yes. The assistant always identifies itself as an automated assistant and can hand the call to a real person whenever the patient asks. It's designed to be helpful, not to trick anyone.
Is this compliant in Australia?
Yes — and it is simpler than most people expect. When a patient fills in your form asking to be contacted, the callback is a solicited call, not telemarketing. Every call still carries valid caller ID, identifies itself as an automated assistant up front, and honours an opt-out on the spot.
What does it cost?
For the first five clinics, the first month is free — no credit card taken. After that it is a flat monthly fee with no lock-in, and you can cancel with a text. Book a call and I'll give you the exact number for your clinic.
What's the catch?
Three things, and they're all free for you too. If the service is exceptional: a Google review, and a line I can put on this website. If it isn't: tell me exactly why — that's worth as much to me. And if you rate it after the month and you know one clinic like yours, one introduction. That's it — no lock-in, no card, cancel with a text.
What if a patient wants to speak to a human?
The assistant offers a human hand-off and books a callback with your team — so no one is ever stuck talking to a bot they don't want to.
Do I need to change my systems?
No. It books into your current calendar and works with the lead sources you already use. Setup is done for you.
How quickly can we be live?
Usually within a week. Most of that is provisioning your clinic's phone number, which carriers review before it goes active.
